# 0. Prerequisites

**Before the workshop, every participant:**

1. **An ElevenLabs account** — https://elevenlabs.io → Sign up (the free plan is enough)
2. **A recording of your own voice (~1 minute)** — phone or computer, in a quiet place; a WAV or MP3 file.
   You will use it for:
   - transcribing your own recording (STT),
   - text-to-speech / voice cloning (TTS),
   - comparing the API vs local Whisper.
3. **A Google account** — for Google Colab (the hands-on part).
4. Optional: a browser with access to https://colab.research.google.com
